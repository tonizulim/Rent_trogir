50cc Scooter Rental in Trogir – Everything You Need to Know

A 50cc scooter is the perfect choice for exploring Trogir old town, Okrug beach, and the quiet roads of Čiovo island. No highway access is needed — these lightweight scooters are ideal for short trips around the Trogir area. All you need is a valid AM or B driving license and you're good to go.

We offer free delivery and pickup for rentals of at least one day in Seget Donji, Vranjica, Trogir and Čiovo, including Okrug Gornji and Okrug Donji. We do not deliver to Split Airport or locations outside these areas. Helmets for driver and passenger are included. A 50cc rental costs €29 for 12 hours, €33 for a full day, or €29 per day on a weekly rental.

Our 50cc fleet includes the Piaggio Fly, Piaggio Typhoon, Vespa Primavera and Derbi Boulevard — all well-maintained and ready to ride. Call or WhatsApp +385 98 780 021 to check availability and book instantly.
